# Compared to Current Agentic Industry

* Yes, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and other providers do give you token counts in their isolated online dashboards - but are they at your fingertips?
* Not only do we allow you to see live usage from within your development workspace, but it offers:
    1. Far more detailed compute tracking (including tokens, compute time, and even thought signatures).
    2. Organization per-Issue instead of per-commit. If it wasn't, it would be hard to aggregate afterwards and very inconvenient because budgets cannot be controlled - and you'd end up with huge, un-payable bills that shut down your business! It is also the most natural way to track thought patterns 
    3. Ability to judge and assign priority, size, and more by tracking the contents of the Issue (vs adjacent ones and even compared to the repo contents themselves).
    4. Out-of-the-box compatability with multiple agent providers (so you are not stuck with having multiple dashboard tabs open to check simple usage that you also have to control manually).
