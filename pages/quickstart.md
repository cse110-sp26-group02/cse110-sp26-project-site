# Overview

This page outlines the steps to quickly get started. 

# Steps

## Brand New User

1. Open the [repo](https://github.com/cse110-sp26-group02/cse110-sp26-project-public). 
2. Click on the `out/` folder.
3. Click on the `release/` folder inside.
4. Click on the `.vsix` file.
5. Select the "download file" button on that page.
6. Once downloaded, keep note of the path where it was downloaded onto your machine and open VS Code.
7. In VS Code, find and click the "Extensions" icon on the left "Activity Bar".
8. Click the 3-dotted line button in the top right of the "sidebar".
9. Select "Install from VSIX...".
10. An extension icon should have appeared in the "Activity Bar". Click on it.
11. If you see "AIT" written in the top left corner of the new interface in the "Sidebar", you have access!
12. Check out the "Configuration" and "Usage" tabs of our site to setup and use our product! Enjoy!
